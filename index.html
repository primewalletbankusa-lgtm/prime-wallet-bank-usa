❓ Frequently Asked Questions

⏳ How long does account approval take?

Account approval typically takes 24–48 hours after successful ID verification. You'll receive an email notification once approved.

💳 How do I unlock Send Money & Pay Bills?

You need to apply for and get approved for a Prime Wallet Card. Go to "Get Card" in your dashboard, submit your details, and wait for admin approval.

💰 Are there any deposit limits?

Basic accounts have a daily limit of $10,000. Fully verified accounts can deposit up to $50,000 daily. Contact support for higher limits.

🔒 How do I reset my PIN or password?

Go to Settings → Security → Change Password. For PIN reset, email us at primewalletbankusa@gmail.com with your full name, email, and ID number.

🌍 Which countries are supported?

Prime Wallet Bank supports users from over 150+ countries worldwide. All major currencies are supported for exchange and transfer.

📞 Need Urgent Help?

Email us directly at primewalletbankusa@gmail.com — we respond 24/7.
