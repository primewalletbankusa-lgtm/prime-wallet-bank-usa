❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 How do I unlock Send Money & Pay Bills? ▼
Purchase a Prime Wallet Card from the "Get Card" page. Once reviewed then approves your card, Send Money & Pay Bills unlock automatically.
⏳ How long does approval take? ▼
Account approval: Within 24 hours. Card approval: Within 48 hours. You'll get email notification once approved.
💳 What cards are accepted for purchase? ▼
Visa, Mastercard, Verve, and American Express are all accepted. Prices start from $20.00.
🔑 Can I change my PIN? ▼
Yes. Go to Settings → Change PIN. You'll need your current PIN to set a new one.
💰 Are there any hidden fees? ▼
No hidden fees. Standard transfers are free. International transfers may have a small processing fee shown before confirmation.
📧 How do I reset my password? ▼
Go to Sign In → Forgot Password. Enter your registered email and account number. Admin will review and respond within 24 hours.
